04 / Perspective

Why this
matters.

Depression can cause clinically significant distress and interfere with school, work, relationships, and daily life.

Understanding which mechanics and implementations resonate with players may help designers and therapists create better, more accessible options alongside professional care.

13.1% of adolescents and adults aged 12+ experienced depression in a two-week period in 2021–2023.

Source: Brody & Hughes, 2025
